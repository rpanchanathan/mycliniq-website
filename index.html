Benefits of My Cliniq

For Patients: Invite-Only Doctors

You decide which doctors join your care team. Bring your own doctor on-board in two taps—no more hunting through endless listings or algorithms steering you toward random providers.

For Patients: Unified Health Records

Auto-link labs, prescriptions & history to every invited doctor. Your data flows seamlessly—you don't have to chase it or manually upload files to multiple portals.

For Patients: SOS & Family Access

Trigger Emergency SOS alerts and grant loved ones secure view-rights. Your safety net is always on demand—multi-channel alerts via app, email, SMS & WhatsApp.

For Doctors: Patient-Driven Invitations

No cold-call outreach needed. Consult patients who have already selected and trusted you. Build your practice with meaningful patient relationships.

For Doctors: Seamless Workflow

Video consults + in-consult notes + auto-generated summaries & follow-ups—all in one unified UI. Rich patient context with intake forms, lab parsing & health-profile analytics.

For Doctors: Automated Billing

Integrated Razorpay payments and earnings dashboard. Focus on care, not invoices. Get notified the moment patients book, message, or need attention.
